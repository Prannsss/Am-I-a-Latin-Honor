USPF Latin Honor Thresholds:

  • Magna Cum Laude: 1.21 – 1.45 (Max 1.75 per academic subject)

Honors GWA Formula
Honors GWA =
∑(Gradei × Academic Unitsi) ∑ Academic Unitsi
Formula Breakdown:
  • Gradei: The numerical final grade earned in an academic course (e.g., 1.25, 1.50).
  • Academic Unitsi: The unit weight assigned to that specific academic course (e.g., 3.0 units).
  • Numerator (∑(Gradei × Academic Unitsi)): Total accumulated quality points across all academic courses.
  • Denominator (∑ Academic Unitsi): Total number of academic credit units earned.

How to Use This System

  1. 1
    Log in to the USPF Student Portal.
    Go to https://uspf.pinnacle.com.ph/uspf/students/ and enter your student credentials.
  2. 2
    Download your Evaluation Form.
    Select the Grades tab. Click Print Course – Curriculum. Save the file to your device (computer or mobile device) as a PDF.
  3. 3
    Upload your Evaluation PDF.
    Click Upload Evaluation PDF on this page. Select the PDF file you saved in Step 2.
  4. 4
    Wait for the system to process your data.
    The system reads the file and fills in your subjects and grades automatically. Do not close the page during this step.
  5. 5
    Enter missing grades.
    After the import, subjects with no grade show a red indicator. Enter the correct grade for each of these subjects.
  6. 6
    (Optional) Export your results.
    Click Export PDF Report to save a printable summary of your academic standing and GWA.
